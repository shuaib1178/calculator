## 2026-06-17 - [Accessible Muted Brand Color]
**Learning:** The 'brand.muted' color (#c0c1c0) failed WCAG AA contrast standards on white backgrounds, providing only a 1.76:1 ratio. This is a common issue when "muted" is interpreted as "light gray" rather than "subtle but readable".
**Action:** Always ensure brand colors used for text have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. Updated 'brand.muted' to #4b5563 (5.43:1 ratio).
