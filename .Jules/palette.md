## 2025-05-17 - Accessible Color Contrast
**Learning:** Ensuring that brand colors used for secondary or muted text meet WCAG AA contrast requirements (at least 4.5:1) is crucial for readability and inclusive design. A small adjustment in a hex code can significantly impact the accessibility of the entire system.
**Action:** Always audit and adjust brand palette colors that fall below accessibility standards. In this case, updating `brand.muted` from `#c0c1c0` to `#6b7280` improved the contrast ratio against white to approximately 4.88:1.
