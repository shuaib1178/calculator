## 2026-06-27 - Improving Brand Token Accessibility
**Learning:** Brand design tokens like `muted` or `secondary` often fail WCAG AA contrast standards if they are chosen purely for aesthetics. Using a tool to verify the contrast ratio (>= 4.5:1 for normal text) against the background is essential for inclusive design.
**Action:** Always check the contrast ratio of brand tokens during exploration. For muted text on a white/off-white background, prefer shades equivalent to gray-600 (#4b5563) or darker to ensure accessibility.
