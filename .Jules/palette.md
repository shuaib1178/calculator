## 2025-06-09 - Accessibility Improvement for Muted Design Tokens
**Learning:** Design tokens used for "muted" text often fail WCAG contrast standards if chosen primarily for aesthetic softness. A contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) is essential for readability, even for secondary or muted content.
**Action:** When defining or auditing "muted" or "secondary" text colors, ensure they meet WCAG AA standards. For a white background (#FFFFFF), a color like #6b7280 (ratio 4.83:1) is a safer choice than lighter greys like #c0c1c0 (ratio 1.94:1).
