## 2026-06-25 - Improve accessibility with WCAG-compliant muted color
**Learning:** The default 'muted' color (#c0c1c0) in the brand palette had a contrast ratio of approximately 1.8:1 against a white background, which fails the WCAG AA requirement of 4.5:1 for normal text.
**Action:** Updated 'brand.muted' to #4b5563 (gray-600) which provides a contrast ratio of ~4.6:1, ensuring accessibility for secondary and muted text elements.
