## 2026-07-11 - Improving Color Contrast for Muted Text
**Learning:** The 'brand.muted' color (#c0c1c0) has a contrast ratio of approximately 1.93:1 against the background (#fcfbfd), which fails WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 for normal text). Darkening it to #4b5563 increases the ratio to ~5.11:1.
**Action:** Always verify contrast ratios for brand colors against the primary background color, especially for 'muted' or 'secondary' shades which are often too light.
