## 2026-06-03 - Improving Color Contrast in Design Tokens
**Learning:** The 'brand.muted' color was initially set to '#c0c1c0', which has a contrast ratio of approximately 1.87:1 against a white background. This fails to meet the WCAG AA minimum requirement of 4.5:1 for normal text. Ensuring that base design tokens meet these standards is critical for overall application accessibility.
**Action:** Updated 'brand.muted' to '#6b7280' to achieve a contrast ratio of 4.83:1, ensuring better readability and compliance with accessibility standards.
