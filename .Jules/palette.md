## 2026-07-04 - [Color Contrast for Muted Text]
**Learning:** WCAG AA compliance for normal text requires a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1. Design tokens like `brand.muted` often use light grays (e.g., #c0c1c0) that fail this requirement on white backgrounds.
**Action:** When defining or updating muted text colors, prefer shades like gray-600 (#4b5563) or darker to ensure accessibility while maintaining the intended visual hierarchy.
