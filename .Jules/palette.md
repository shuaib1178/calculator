## 2026-07-03 - Improved Color Contrast for Muted Text
**Learning:** The previous `brand.muted` color (#c0c1c0) had a contrast ratio of approximately 1.91:1 against a white background, which fails WCAG AA standards (minimum 4.5:1). Darkening it to #4b5563 ensures readability for all users while maintaining a "muted" aesthetic.
**Action:** Always check the contrast ratio of brand-specific "muted" or "disabled" colors against their intended background using WCAG AA standards.
