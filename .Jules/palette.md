# Palette's Journal - Critical Learnings

## 2025-05-15 - Color Contrast Improvement
**Learning:** The 'brand.muted' color (#c0c1c0) in 'tailwind.config.js' had a contrast ratio of 1.81:1 against white backgrounds, failing WCAG AA standards.
**Action:** Updated 'brand.muted' to #6b7280, which provides a 4.83:1 contrast ratio, ensuring better readability and accessibility for secondary text elements.
