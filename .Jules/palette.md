## 2026-07-03 - Accessible Muted Color Contrast
**Learning:** The default muted color #c0c1c0 fails WCAG AA contrast requirements (4.5:1) for normal text against white backgrounds, making it difficult for users with visual impairments to read secondary information.
**Action:** Always use a color with at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, such as #4b5563 (Tailwind's gray-600), for muted text to ensure accessibility without sacrificing the desired aesthetic hierarchy.
