## 2026-06-21 - [Accessible Muted Color Fix]
**Learning:** The 'brand.muted' color (#c0c1c0) in the Tailwind theme had a contrast ratio of only 1.86:1 against a white background, failing WCAG AA standards (minimum 4.5:1).
**Action:** Updated 'brand.muted' to #4b5563, which provides a contrast ratio of 5.11:1, ensuring better accessibility for secondary text and decorative elements while maintaining visual hierarchy.
